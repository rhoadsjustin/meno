Memorize the Christmas story by Christmas

Luke 2:1–14, a few verses each week of Advent. On Christmas morning, say it from memory.

Four weeks

  1. Nov 29Luke 2:1–4The journey to Bethlehem
  2. Dec 6Luke 2:5–7The birth
  3. Dec 13Luke 2:8–11Good news of great joy
  4. Dec 20Luke 2:12–14Glory to God in the highest, then the whole passage together
  5. Dec 25Luke 2:1–14From memory
