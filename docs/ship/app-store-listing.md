## Description (4000 chars max)

> Meno helps you memorize Scripture — and keep it.
>
> Pick a passage: a single verse, a psalm, or whole chapters. Meno breaks it
> into small, day-sized pieces and walks each one up a ladder of practice
> modes:
>
> • Read — settle into the words
> • First letters — recite from a letter cipher
> • Fill in the blanks — 25%, 50%, then 75% of the words removed
> • Arrange — rebuild the passage from shuffled phrases
> • Type — write it from memory
> • Speak — recite it aloud, checked on your device
>
> As a verse solidifies in memory, its text visibly dissolves from the
> screen — first letters, then just the reference. That's the whole idea:
> it lives in you, not the app.
>
> KEEP IT FRESH
> Memorized verses come back for spaced-repetition reviews right before
> you'd forget them. Optional pop quizzes arrive out of the blue (never
> more than two a day, never at night) — thirty seconds that keep a verse
> yours for good. Stitch sessions regularly ask you to recite everything
> so far as one passage, so chapters hold together, not just fragments.
>
> RECITE TO UNLOCK
> Choose apps you'd rather open less, and Meno will ask you to recite your
> current verse before they open. It's a door, not a wall: an override
> button always works, instantly, no questions asked. Your app choices
> never leave your device — Apple's Screen Time framework keeps them
> private even from us.
>
> ALWAYS IN FRONT OF YOU
> Home screen and Lock Screen widgets show your current verse at its
> current level of dissolution, along with your streak and due reviews.
>
> FOUR TRANSLATIONS
> World English Bible, King James Version, and American Standard Version
> are built in and work fully offline. The English Standard Version (ESV®)
> is fetched once per passage and kept offline, used by permission of
> Crossway.
>
> QUIETLY YOURS
> No account. No ads. No analytics. Everything — your goals, your progress,
> your recitations — stays on your phone. Recitation audio is processed
> entirely on-device and never recorded or uploaded.
>
> Meno (μένω): to remain, to abide. "If you abide in me, and my words abide
> in you…" — John 15:7
